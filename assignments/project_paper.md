# Overview

This is part of the team project. You will also present your project to the class during what would be the final exam period.

**This is a team assignment. When you are done:**
* ***One* member of your team should submit the files, including the names of everyone who contributed.**
* **Everyone else should turn in an empty assignment on Google Classroom, including a comment about who turned in the document for your team.**

# Paper
Your team's final paper should be 6-8 pages long. Your paper should provide background, method, results, and discussion for your application of deep learning to real data. The intended audience is "other students in this class".

Address the same questions as in the [outline step](project_outline.md) of the project.

Your code and raw data should all be in a single directory on BLT. Include a single, clean script that someone could use to reproduce your experiment. If you need multiple scripts (e.g., because preprocessing is a compute-intensive step that you only wanted to run once), explain this. Include a file `README.txt` that explains in detail how to run your entire experiment on BLT.

# What to Hand in
***One member of your team*** should hand in:
* Your paper as a Google Doc, Word document, PDF, or .odf file. Remember to include the names of everyone who worked on it.
* The path to the directory on BLT where your code, data, and `README.txt` file are located.

***Everyone else*** should turn in an empty assignment on Google Classroom, including a comment about who turned in the document for your team.
