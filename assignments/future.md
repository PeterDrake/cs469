# Overview
This is an opportunity to look back and forward, thinking about where AI is going.

**This is an individual assignment. You are meant to write the text on your own. You are welcome to discuss *ideas* with other students (including on the class email list).**

# Predicting the Future
Write 2-3 pages (single spaced) about where you think AI is going in the next five years. Some topics you might consider:

* How widely will it be used?
* What will (and won't) it be able to do?
* Some people currently despise generative AI, while others embrace it. Will one side "win", or will there be some kind of reconciliation?
* How will AI be regulated?
* How will people work with it?
* How will human work change? What skills will remain or become useful for humans?
* What will happen with the business of AI, including companies, data centers, and prices?
* How will AI affect society -- art, leisure, interactions with other people, politics, education, etc.?
* Will general superintelligence arise? If so, what are the consequences of that?

You don't need to be correct (we won't know until we get there) or agree with my guesses. I'm looking for opinions informed by what we've studied and talked about in class. Put another way, I want an essay different from what you would have written at the beginning of the semester!
