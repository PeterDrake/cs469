# Overview

This is part of the team project. Over the course of the semester, your team will build a neural network model for a machine learning task, train it on data, and evaluate it on test data.

**This is a team assignment. When you are done:**
* ***One* member of your team should submit the file, including (as a comment at the top of the file) the names of everyone who contributed.**
* **Everyone else should turn in an empty assignment on Google Classroom, including a comment about who turned in the document for your team.**

# Questions
Answer the following questions, adapted from subsection 6.1.1 of Chollet and Watson:
1. What project has your team decided on?
2. What will your input data be?
3. What are you trying to predict or generate?
4. Identify a source for the training data you'll need. You don't have to actually download the data yet, but be able to point to some files that you think will work.
5. What type of task is this (e.g., binary classification)?
6. How would someone do this task without using machine learning? (If existing machine learning solutions exist, I don't expect you to beat the current state of the art.)
7. What ethical concerns, if any, are there about building or using your system?

# What to Hand In
***One member of your team*** should hand in a single document answering the questions above. Include the ***full names*** of everyone who worked on it.

***Everyone else*** should turn in an empty assignment on Google Classroom, including a comment about who turned in the document for your team.
