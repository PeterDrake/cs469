# Overview
This is part of the team project. Over the course of the semester, your team will build a neural network model for a machine learning task, train it on data, and evaluate it on test data.

**This is a team assignment. When you are done:**
* ***One* member of your team should submit the file, including (as a comment at the top of the file) the names of everyone who contributed.**
* **Everyone else should turn in an empty assignment on Google Classroom, including a comment about who turned in the document for your team.**

# Outline and Initial Results
Your training and validation should be essentially done at this stage, although you may still refine it before completing your paper. It would be appropriate to report validation error rates at this point even if you plan to do more hyperparameter tuning.

Write an outline of your team's final paper, which will eventually be 6-8 pages long. The intended audience is "other students in this class". Things to consider:

Things to consider:
* What background does the reader need?
* What task is your network learning to do?
* How are you measuring success?
* How well does your network perform?
* What training data did you use and why?
* What is your network architecture?
* Can your network beat a trivial baseline?
* Is overfitting an issue?
* What are the limitations of your system? For example, where might it produce incorrect answers or be inappropriate to use?
